Historical scholarship

Dates, figures and biographical facts throughout the site reflect widely available public scholarship on Partition. Several statistics (death toll, number displaced) remain genuinely disputed among historians and are presented here as ranges for that reason. Further reading referenced directly in the Women & Children and Train to Pakistan carriages:

  • Urvashi Butalia: The Other Side of Silence: Voices from the Partition of India
  • Ritu Menon & Kamla Bhasin: Borders and Boundaries: Women in India's Partition
  • Khushwant Singh: Train to Pakistan (Chatto & Windus, 1956)
  • Saadat Hasan Manto: short stories, including Toba Tek Singh
  • Bhisham Sahni: Tamas
  • Bapsi Sidhwa: Cracking India
  • Amrita Pritam: Pinjar and selected poetry
